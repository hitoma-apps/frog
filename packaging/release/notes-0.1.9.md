# FROG 0.1.9

## Changes

- Archive, Trash, Star, read state and the label controls are shown but disabled until you check a thread.
- The details marker for IDs is ⓘ.
- A selected sidebar row's highlight covers its arrow too. The arrows are larger, and the highlight no longer covers the first letter of a name.
- Settings → General has a reading font: Monospace (the default), Sans-serif, or Serif.

## Downloads

- **Linux (x86_64):** `frog.AppImage`. Installed copies of 0.1.0 and later update themselves to this version.
- **macOS (universal):** `FROG-macos-universal.zip`. Not yet signed with a Developer ID, so macOS asks you to approve it on first open. Move the app to Applications first.
- **Windows (x86_64):** `frog.exe`. Not yet code-signed. Automatic updates on Windows wait for signing.
