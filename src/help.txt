== Search operators ==

There are none yet. Type words in the search box, then press Enter or click elsewhere to run the search.

- Every word must match, so "invoice march" finds mail with both words.
- A word matches the start of a word in the sender or the subject.
- Searches cover the sender and subject only, not message bodies.
- Operators such as "from:" are not supported yet. They're planned in issue 51.

== Saved and recent searches ==

- "Save search" in the toolbar keeps the current search under Searches in the sidebar. Up to 10 are kept.
- "Most recent" under Searches lists the last five searches, saved or not.

== Saving mail ==

- "Save .eml" in the reader writes each message of the conversation as its own file.
- "Save text" writes the whole conversation as one text file.
- Files go to your Downloads folder. The status bar shows where they went.
