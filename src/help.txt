== Search ==

Type words or operators in the search box, then press Enter or click elsewhere. Results are messages, newest first. Click one to open its conversation.

- Words must all match the sender or the subject, as whole words or the start of a word.
- from:alice      the sender contains alice
- subject:tax     the subject contains tax
- label:Work      the message has the label Work. Use label:"Work stuff" for a name with spaces.
- is:unread, is:read
- has:attachment
- after:2026-01-31, before:2026-01-31   received on or after, or before, that day (your local time)
- Every part must match the same message.
- Only cached messages are searched, not message bodies. Quoted phrases, "-" to exclude and OR are not supported yet.

== Saved and recent searches ==

- "Save search" in the toolbar keeps the current search under Searches in the sidebar. Up to 10 are kept.
- "Most recent" under Searches lists the last five searches, saved or not.

== Saving mail ==

- "Save .eml" in the reader writes each message of the conversation as its own file.
- "Save text" writes the whole conversation as one text file.
- Files go to your Downloads folder. The status bar shows where they went.
